Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to your Al-Andalus account.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Account Details.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the warning and confirm Delete Account.

The signed-in account and associated personal data are deleted after confirmation.

Request assistance

If you cannot access the app, contact us through WhatsApp at +964 780 173 0506 or email salamadil1233@gmail.com. We may verify account ownership before processing the request.

Data that is deleted

  • Account profile information and saved delivery or contact details
  • Pet, veterinary, vaccine, and deworming records connected to the account
  • Uploaded animal images or files connected to the account, where technically available
  • Account notifications and app records that are no longer required

Data that may be retained

Limited order, payment, invoice, support, security, and audit records may be retained when required for legal, financial, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or operational compliance obligations.

Processing and retention period

In-app deletion is processed immediately after confirmation. Support-assisted requests are normally processed within 30 days. Limited records are retained only as long as required, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

Delete some data without deleting the account

You may request correction or deletion of specific app data through the support channels above without deleting your entire account.
